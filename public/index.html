Product Guide

Clear guidance for the product you purchased.

1. Follow the product label

If you purchased NervLief, the listed serving is 2 capsules per day. Follow the instructions on the bottle and do not exceed two capsules per day. Do not take extra doses.

2. Build a consistent routine

Taking the product at the same time each day can help you remember your routine.

3. Take with water

Take with a full glass of water unless your healthcare professional advises otherwise.

4. Store your bottle correctly

Store in a cool, dry place out of reach of children.

Important Notice This information is educational and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional if you have a medical condition, take medication, or experience worsening symptoms.

Install My Natural Care

Add this app to your phone home screen for easy access.

🍎 iPhone / iPad

  1. Open mynaturalcare.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share.
  3. Select Add to Home Screen and then tap Add.

📱 Android

  1. Open mynaturalcare.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ or Install.
  3. Select Install App ou Add to Home Screen.

Once installed, open it from your home screen. Your email will stay saved on this device.
