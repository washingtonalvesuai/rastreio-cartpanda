Simple Daily Habits for Nerve Health

Small, practical steps that support your overall health alongside the care recommended by your clinician.

🥗

Build balanced meals

Include vegetables, fruit, beans, whole grains, and protein such as eggs, fish, or poultry. These foods provide nutrients that support general health; no single food cures neuropathy.

🚶

Move safely each day

Try gentle walking or chair-based movement if safe for you. If you have reduced foot sensation, balance difficulties or wounds, ask a clinician which activities are appropriate.

🦶

Check your feet

Look for cuts, blisters, redness, or swelling every day, especially if you have diabetes or numbness. Wear comfortable shoes, and avoid walking barefoot when feeling is reduced.

😴

Make rest a priority

Keep regular sleep and wake times, and build a calm bedtime routine. Let your healthcare team know if discomfort keeps you awake.

💧

Stay comfortably hydrated

Drink water throughout the day as appropriate for your health. If you have heart or kidney conditions requiring fluid limits, follow your clinician's guidance.

❤️

Care for the bigger picture

Avoid smoking, limit alcohol, keep up with checkups, and manage blood sugar with your medical team if you have diabetes.

An easy daily routine

☀️

Morning

Eat a balanced breakfast, review the directions on your bottle, and check your feet if sensation is reduced.

🌤️

Afternoon

Enjoy a nutritious lunch, drink water as appropriate, and do a little gentle movement suited to your abilities.

🌙

Evening

Choose a comfortable dinner, inspect your feet if needed, and maintain a relaxing bedtime routine.

🩺

Whenever you need help

Contact a clinician for new or worsening symptoms, injuries, redness, swelling or wounds. Seek prompt help for suspected infection.

About vitaminsMore is not always better. Too much supplemental vitamin B6 can itself damage nerves. Before combining supplements, ask your healthcare professional to review your total vitamin intake.
