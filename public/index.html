How to Use Your Product

Simple instructions for your purchase. Always follow the directions on your bottle.

1. Follow the label directions

If you purchased NervLief, the previous guide lists 2 capsules daily. Check the instructions on your current bottle and do not exceed the recommended amount.

2. Build a steady routine

Taking the product at the same time each day makes it easier to remember and improves consistency.

3. Take with water

Use a full glass of water unless your healthcare professional gave you different instructions.

4. Keep the bottle safe

Store in a cool, dry place and keep out of reach of children.

Important reminder This guidance is educational only. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If the customer has a medical condition, uses medication, or notices worsening symptoms, they should speak with a healthcare professional.

Healthy Habits That Can Help

Everyday ideas to support your well-being, alongside professional medical guidance.

Nourishing foods

Include a balanced routine with vegetables, fruit, lean proteins, eggs, fish, beans, whole grains and healthy fats, according to the person’s needs and clinician guidance.

Gentle movement

Short walks, stretching and low-impact movement may help support circulation and mobility when appropriate.

Foot care

For people with numbness or diabetes, check the feet daily for cuts, redness, blisters or swelling, and wear comfortable shoes.

Sleep and hydration

Good sleep and regular hydration can support general well-being. Follow medical guidance if fluid limits apply.

Easy daily routine

MorningBalanced breakfast, water, follow the label directions, and check your feet if sensation is reduced.
AfternoonNutritious lunch, gentle movement if appropriate, and stay hydrated.
EveningComfortable dinner, quiet bedtime routine, and contact support or a clinician if something feels worse.
Helpful references: NIDDK — Foot Care · NHS — Peripheral Neuropathy · NIH — Vitamin B6
